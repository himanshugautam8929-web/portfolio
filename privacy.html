Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Anmol Gautam, freelance web & app developer, Ghaziabad, India.

What this site collects

This is a simple portfolio website. It has no accounts, no sign-ups and no newsletter. It collects almost nothing on its own:

  • When you message me on WhatsApp (the "Chat on WhatsApp" buttons and the Project Brief Builder), the details you type — your name, business name and message — are sent to me through WhatsApp, and are governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy. I use them only to reply to you and to discuss your project. I do not sell or share them.
  • Cookie choice: if you dismiss the cookie notice, your choice is stored in your own browser (localStorage) so the notice does not keep reappearing. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Fonts: the site loads its two fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when the page loads, as with any site using Google Fonts.
  • Analytics (when switched on): the site is set up for Google Analytics, but it is not active until a measurement ID is added. Once active, it will collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (pages visited, device type, approximate location). You can block it with any tracker blocker.

What I do with your details

Only what is needed to answer you and deliver your project — preparing a quote, building your website, and supporting it afterwards. I do not sell, rent or trade personal details, and I do not send marketing messages unless you ask for them.

Your choices

You can ask me at any time — on WhatsApp — to delete the conversation or any details you shared, and I will do so. You can also clear this site's stored cookie choice from your browser settings.
