06 — Questions
What everyone asks first.

    

      
How does payment work? Why an advance?
50% advance by UPI, and the remaining 50% only after your website is live. The advance keeps both of us committed — and my rule is simple: if the work cannot begin, or the design direction does not satisfy you, the advance is returned.

      
How long will my website take?
A landing page takes 3–5 days and a business website 5–7 days. For a custom app, I give you the timeline after understanding the scope — a system like InstitutionOne is weeks of work, and I would rather promise honestly than promise quickly.

      
Who provides the domain and hosting?
We register them in your name (approximately ₹1,500–2,500 per year) so the website remains your property, always — I handle the complete setup. In some cases we can begin on free hosting and decide together on WhatsApp.

      
What if I need changes later?
The Business Website package includes one month of small changes, free. After that, I continue to make small edits for a nominal charge — you never need to touch any code yourself.

      
You're a student — why should I trust you?
A fair question, and it has a verifiable answer: open InstitutionOne, live — a real app approved for an entire college. Beyond that, half your payment is due only after delivery. We first speak for free and you see a design direction — then you decide.

      
I'm not in Ghaziabad — will this still work?
Completely. Everything is handled over WhatsApp and calls. If you are in Ghaziabad or Delhi NCR, we can also meet in person.
