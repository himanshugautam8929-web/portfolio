About us

A home bakery that bakes the healthy way

Fresh Bakes by Meera is an eggless home bakery in Sector 62, Noida. Everything is baked fresh in a home kitchen — healthy tea cakes, cookies, brownies and breads, made with wholesome ingredients and no chemicals or preservatives.
