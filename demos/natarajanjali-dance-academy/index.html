About us

Art, culture & tradition

Natarajanjali Dance Academy by Ipshita Bhattacharyya is a classical dance academy in Noida, teaching Bharatanatyam and semi-classical dance to kids and ladies.

Our motto is Learn, Perform, Grow — students learn the art with discipline, perform on stage, and grow with every class.
