About us

A little cafe with a lot of flavour

GREEN & BEANS is a tucked-away cafe in downtown Houston, TX, on Louisiana St — a favourite lunch stop for downtown workers.

Alongside coffee and seasonal drinks, the compact counter serves kimbap, garlic chicken bowls, yogurt bowls and fresh cookies — quick, fresh and full of flavour.
