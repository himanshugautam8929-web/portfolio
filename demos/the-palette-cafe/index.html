About Us

A cafe where you make the art

Most cafes ask you to sit and eat. Here you sit, eat — and paint. Choose a 3D miniature, a canvas, a tote bag or a string art board, pick your colours, and create something to take home.

It works for everyone: a date that is actually fun, a family afternoon without screens, or a quiet hour to yourself with a coffee and a brush.
