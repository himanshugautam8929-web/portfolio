About us

A quiet cafe, made for lingering

Seeds and Soul Coffee is a cafe in Spring Hill, Brisbane, serving specialty coffee and brunch in a bright, relaxed space.

Rotating single-origin beans sit beside a brunch menu with favourites like the Original Acai Bowl and the Seeds and Soul Eggs Benny — with vegan and vegetarian options too.
