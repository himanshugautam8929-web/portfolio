About us

Couture cakes, baked with love

Studio Cake O’ Luv is a home bakery and cake studio in Noida, run by cake artist Megha Kwatra Madan. We make custom couture cakes, wedding cakes, cheesecakes, cupcakes and dessert tables — each one designed for your occasion.

We also teach — baking and cake classes for aspiring bakers, online and at the studio.
