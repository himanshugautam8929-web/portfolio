About us

Homely food, baked fresh every day

Siya’s Kitchen and Cafe is a warm, welcoming bakery and cafe in Raj Nagar, Ghaziabad. We deal in all types of eggless cakes and vegetarian food — from beautifully decorated celebration cakes to everyday comfort food.

Drop in for a coffee, order a cake for your special day, or get your favourites delivered. Dine-in, takeaway and delivery available.
