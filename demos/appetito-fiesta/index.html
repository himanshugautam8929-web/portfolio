About Us

A cafe that is also a playground

Appetito Fiesta brings food and play together. Inside, a warm dining hall serves Italian, Chinese, Asian, Thai and Lebanese dishes. Outside, the courtyard turns into ParView Mini Golf, pickleball courts and the Bikonic pump track.

Families come for lunch and stay for the games; friends come for the games and stay for dinner. Either way, it is a full day out in one place.
