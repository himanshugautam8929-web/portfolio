About us

A little coffee hatch with a big heart

Coffee Academy is an independent artisan cafe and doughnut spot at Oxo Tower Wharf on Bargehouse Street, London — a stop for Caravan roast coffee and fresh Crosstown doughnuts.

Pop by for a coffee and a doughnut, then take a short stroll to the benches along the River Thames.
