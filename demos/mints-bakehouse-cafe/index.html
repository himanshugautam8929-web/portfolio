About us

A little celebration for every sweet tooth

Mints bakehouse & Cafe sits on the ground floor of Radisson Blu, Greater Noida — a bright, relaxed cafe and bakery serving freshly baked artisan sweets, savoury treats and ethically sourced coffee.

Come for a slow breakfast, a coffee break, afternoon tea or a celebration cake — or simply pick up something sweet on your way.
